# Budget — $27,000 over 8 months, paid per milestone

Rationale for the shape of this budget:

- Recent ZCG decisions show approvals clustering at roughly $1.5k–$40k with
  milestone-based payments and skepticism toward large asks from new teams.
- No hardware or cloud costs are requested for M1–M2 (PoC and prototype run
  anywhere); the M3 pilot node hosting is line-itemed explicitly.

## Payment schedule

Each milestone is paid on completion and acceptance of its deliverables
(defined in [PROPOSAL.md](PROPOSAL.md) §4). No upfront payment is requested.

## What is explicitly NOT requested

- Salaries for roles that do not exist yet, equipment, travel.
- Marketing, market research, or event budgets.
- Tokens/price exposure — all amounts are USD-denominated for budgeting;
  ZCG handles disbursement mechanics.

## Value comparison

For context, the last ZCG meeting cycle (Oct 2026) approved a $1,500 community
project and declined a $38,000 receive-bridge and a $61,400 custom-circuit
proposal. This ask — $27,000 for four verifiable milestones against an open RFP —
sits inside the proven approval envelope with acceptance criteria the committee
can check mechanically.
